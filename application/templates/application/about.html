About us

From first Chinese words to confident full sentences.

Learn Chinese is built around one simple idea: language learning becomes powerful when it becomes a daily habit. The free path helps beginners start speaking, then Premium continues the journey into grammar, work, culture, and longer spoken answers.

1

Words, pinyin, and tones

New learners begin with sound, pronunciation, greetings, numbers, and the first words they can say out loud.

2

Daily sentences

Students turn vocabulary into useful sentences for introductions, shopping, restaurants, and simple questions.

3

Real situations

Travel and daily conversation lessons help learners speak in practical scenes instead of only memorizing lists.

4

Grammar and longer speech

After the free foundation, Premium lessons teach sentence building, connectors, and full spoken responses.

5

Business and culture

Learners continue into professional Chinese, culture, poetry, idioms, and more expressive communication.

Free foundation

  • HSK 1 Starter Course for pronunciation, vocabulary, and basic word order.
  • Daily Conversation for greetings, shopping, restaurants, and personal questions.
  • Travel Chinese Essentials for hotels, directions, food, and emergency phrases.

Premium continuation

We continue to offer business Chinese and Chinese culture-related content. According to our Chinese teachers, this content will better help learners learn Chinese, but it may require a course subscription.
