About us

Making Chinese learning simple, practical, and enjoyable.

Learn Chinese is designed for learners from around the world. Whether you are starting from zero or improving Chinese for travel, study, or work, you can find a course path that fits your goal.

Our courses

  • HSK beginner vocabulary and basic grammar.
  • Daily conversation for greetings, shopping, restaurants, transport, and hotels.
  • Travel Chinese for airports, directions, hotels, and emergency phrases.
  • Premium business Chinese for meetings, email, and professional communication.

How learning works

Start with free lessons, then use quizzes to check your understanding. When you are ready to go further, Premium courses give you a more complete path for advanced practice. Our goal is to help every learner speak Chinese with more confidence.
